# 📊 Customer Churn Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end Data Analytics project designed to identify customer churn patterns, evaluate customer lifetime value (CLV), and generate actionable business insights for improving customer retention in the telecom industry.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by telecom companies. Losing existing customers directly impacts revenue and growth.

This project leverages SQL, Python, and Power BI to analyze customer behavior, identify churn drivers, measure customer lifetime value, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer churn behavior
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Calculate and monitor churn rate
- Evaluate Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Discover key churn drivers
- Support customer retention strategies
- Deliver interactive business intelligence dashboards

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

✔ Customers on Month-to-Month contracts exhibit the highest churn rates.

✔ Customers with lower tenure are more likely to leave.

✔ Fiber Optic subscribers show higher churn behavior compared to other internet services.

✔ Customers without Online Security and Tech Support services have increased churn probability.

✔ Long-term contracts significantly improve customer retention.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Promote long-term contract plans.
- Offer retention incentives for new customers.
- Bundle security and support services.
- Create targeted campaigns for high-risk customers.
- Monitor churn KPIs continuously.

## Key Business Insights

* Identified customer segments with the highest churn risk based on contract type, tenure, and service usage.
* Analyzed customer lifetime value (CLV) to understand long-term revenue contribution.
* Evaluated the impact of internet services, security features, and support plans on customer retention.
* Discovered that customers on shorter contracts exhibit significantly higher churn rates compared to long-term subscribers.
* Highlighted opportunities for improving retention through targeted customer engagement and service optimization.

## Business Impact

This analysis enables organizations to proactively identify at-risk customers, reduce customer attrition, improve retention strategies, and maximize customer lifetime value. The dashboard provides decision-makers with actionable insights to support data-driven business growth.
